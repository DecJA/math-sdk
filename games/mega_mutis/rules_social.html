Mega Multis Gameplay Rules

Game Overview

  • Maximum award: 5000x the underlying spin amount.
  • Base mode: Can be played for 1.0x the underlying spin amount.
  • Feature mode: Can be played for 100x the underlying spin amount.

Game Rules

For all game modes, 9 hidden tiles are displayed on screen. Click on a tile to reveal an individual award multiplier. Match 3 identical multipliers to be awarded that amount. The award is calculated as:

Final Award = Spin Amount × Award Multiplier

Base Mode

  • Return to Player (RTP): 96.00%
  • Awards range from 0.1x to 5000.0x
  • One spin action reveals 9 tiles

Feature Mode

  • Return to Player (RTP): 96.00%
  • Awards range from 5.0x to 5000.0x
  • One spin action reveals 9 tiles

UI Controls

  • Spin Button: Start a spin. The amount shown under "Spin Amount" will be deducted from your coin balance.
  • Fastplay: Automatically reveals all 9 tiles and highlights awarded results without additional clicks.
  • Autospin: Play multiple rounds automatically. All tiles are revealed each round without manual interaction.
  • Mute: Toggle all game audio on/off.
  • Feature Spins: Open the modal to activate the 100x Feature Mode. Once activated, each spin deducts 100x the underlying spin amount from your coin balance.
