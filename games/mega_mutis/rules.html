Mega Multis Gameplay Rules

Game Overview

  • Maximum payout: 5000x the underlying bet.
  • Base mode: Costs 1.0x the underlying bet.
  • Bonus mode: Costs 100x the underlying bet.

Game Rules

For all game modes, 9 hidden tiles are displayed on screen. Click on a tile to reveal an individual payout multiplier. Match 3 identical multipliers to win that prize. The payout is calculated as:

Final Payout = Bet Amount × Payout Multiplier

Base Mode

  • Return to Player (RTP): 96.00%
  • Payouts range from 0.1x to 5000.0x
  • One bet action reveals 9 tiles

UI Controls

  • Bet Button: Place a bet. The amount shown under "Bet Amount" will be deducted from your balance.
  • Fastplay: Automatically reveals all 9 tiles and highlights wins without additional clicks.
  • Autobet: Play multiple rounds automatically. All tiles are revealed each round without manual interaction.
  • Mute: Toggle all game audio on/off.
  • Bonus: Open the modal to activate the 100x Bonus Mode. Once activated, each bet deducts 100x the underlying bet amount from your balance.
